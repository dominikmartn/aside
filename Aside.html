Aside 1.7.11

Agents never see your secrets, and recordings survive moody audio devices.

Agent access

  • Aside scans your notes before agents can read them. Turning on agent access now checks the vault for things that look like API keys, tokens, or passwords, and shows you what it found before anything is shared.
  • Secrets are redacted either way. Anything secret-shaped is masked in every response an agent gets, even in notes written after the scan.

Recording

  • No more cryptic errors when your mic isn't ready. When a Bluetooth headset is still switching profiles, Aside now quietly retries instead of failing with a technical message.
  • Aside notices when system audio goes dead. If the other side of a call stops arriving, you get a banner and the connection is rebuilt automatically instead of recording a one-sided meeting.
