Aside 2.0.1

fixes after the 2.0.0 launch: the first run, dictation on more microphones, and a trial count that holds.

first run

  • your mac stays usable while the setup runs. the menu bar, force quit and your other apps all stay reachable.
  • checking for updates during the setup no longer freezes aside.
  • the setup opens on the display you are working on, and the panel clears the notch afterwards.
  • the dictation step has a way on when the speech model will not load, and esc no longer skips the setup.
  • a link you click before the setup is finished waits for it instead of opening a window behind it.

dictation

  • a microphone on the second input channel is heard, and aside follows the loudest input instead of summing all of them.
  • a take with no speech in it is dropped instead of inserting words nobody said.
  • aside keeps waiting for a slow speech model instead of blaming the download, and esc reaches an input method that is still composing.
  • changing the microphone while a recording starts no longer quits aside.

trial and licence

  • the free meetings you have used survive a reinstall, and a failed write never hands them back.
  • an address that cannot receive a licence is refused straight away, with the reason.

notes

  • a summary that failed keeps its retry across meetings, and a short meeting is no longer reported as a failed ai call.
  • aside asks before sending crash reports, and says that hangs and unclean exits go with them.
