Aside 1.6.0

Your meeting notes are now a real document. Write in them, restructure them, drop things into them.

New

  • Notes you can actually write in. Press Return for a new paragraph, type - for a bullet, ## for a heading, [] for a checkbox. The markdown characters disappear into what they mean as you type.
  • The formatting you expect. Bold, italic, inline code, links, quotes, numbered lists and nested lists, plus dividers. Tab and Shift-Tab nest a list item, Return twice leaves it.
  • Drag files onto a note. Images appear inline; documents become a page-shaped card you can click for a Quick Look preview. Everything is copied into your vault, so the note stays whole even if you move the original.
  • Right-click for formatting. Every block type with its keyboard shortcut, and a tick next to the one you are in.
  • Select, undo and search across the whole note. Selection spans blocks, undo covers structure changes, and Find works in the note.

Fixed

  • Blank lines and indentation survive. Opening a note no longer flattens its spacing or nesting.
  • Headings look like headings. Three sizes instead of one, in the notes list and in the panel alike.
  • The page no longer jumps. Ticking a checkbox halfway down a note keeps you where you were.
  • Notes stopped repainting constantly. An open note was redrawing itself every frame, which showed up as a flickering text cursor and burnt battery for nothing.
