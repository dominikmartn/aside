Aside 1.7.6

Tag many meetings at once, pick your Copilot provider from a proper menu, and icons you can actually see.

New

  • Tag several meetings in one step. Select multiple meetings in the library (Cmd- or Shift-click), then tag them all at once — from the right-click menu or the field in the selection pane. Existing tags are suggested as you type.
  • Provider menu in Copilot settings. Bringing your own model now picks the provider from a dropdown with full names instead of a cramped row of segments.

Improved

  • Bolder icons everywhere. Every outline icon moved to a heavier stroke, and the Settings sidebar now uses solid glyphs — small icons stay readable at small sizes.
  • Round buttons are one consistent control now, with slightly larger glyphs — the settings lens, copy, share, delete and the transcript player all match.
