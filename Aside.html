Aside 1.6.3

A small, fast fix for a very visible bug: the meeting pill at the notch could flicker wildly. Thanks to the tester who filmed it.

Fixed

  • The notch pill no longer flickers. On some display scalings, resting the pointer near the pill's lower edge made the whole bar rapidly jump up and down. Hovering no longer changes the bar's size, so the jitter is gone.
  • Only one Aside runs at a time. Launching a new copy while an old one was still running (say, opening an update straight from the download) left two invisible instances fighting over the notch — another way to get that flicker. The newest launch now takes over and the old one quits cleanly.
