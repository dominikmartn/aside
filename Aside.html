Ocelli 2.2

A new speech engine. Dictation and transcripts are now much faster.

  • Ocelli now transcribes with NVIDIA Parakeet. Dictation is ready almost instantly after launch and needs far less memory.
  • Each dictation goes to the right model by itself: Parakeet for its 25 European languages, Whisper for everything else. You can still pick Whisper for everything in Settings › General › Speech.
  • English meetings are transcribed by Parakeet. Meetings in other languages get their final transcript from Whisper, which Ocelli downloads in the background if it isn't on your Mac yet.
  • Updates now wait while you record, dictate or while a note is still being written.
  • Opening Ocelli straight from the disk image now moves it to Applications for you.
  • This update is larger than usual because it includes the new speech model.
