Aside 2.0.4

added a microphone picker, a feedback form and automatic deletion of old recordings, and made recording hold up when the mic misbehaves or nobody is talking.

new

  • pick a microphone for meetings in settings › calls and a separate one for dictation in settings › dictation. if the one you picked is unplugged, aside uses the system default until it's back.
  • dictation can use apple's on-device speech recognition for english: pick it under settings › dictation › speech recognition. automatic stays on whisper.
  • send feedback straight from the app: "send feedback" at the bottom of the settings sidebar.
  • delete recordings automatically after 7, 30 or 90 days in settings › privacy. only the audio goes, your notes and transcripts stay. off unless you turn it on.

recording

  • when the microphone stops responding, stop still finishes within seconds and the note keeps what was recorded.
  • a microphone that changes in the middle of a meeting no longer stops the whole recording.
  • the call buffer stops retrying a microphone that won't open, instead of trying again all call long and keeping airpods in phone mode.

transcript

  • waiting alone in a silent call no longer fills the live transcript with "thank you."
  • the first seconds of the live transcript are in the language you speak, not in english.

notes

  • a recording with only a few words says "too short to summarize" and keeps its transcript, instead of getting a made-up summary.
  • a meeting you share as a file now looks like an aside page.

aside ai

  • in a country aside ai can't serve, aside now says so and points you to your own api key or ollama, instead of calling your key broken.

settings

  • a notes folder that is gone for good can be forgotten in settings › vault, so the recordings that only belonged to it get cleaned up.
