Ocelli 2.4

A mirror before your meetings, dictations up to 30 minutes, and updates that install themselves.

  • A mirror before your calls. When the pill shows an upcoming meeting, it has a camera button. Click it and a round mirror opens under the pill. Only you see it, nothing is recorded, and it never turns on the microphone. Pick a camera there, or turn on Ring light in a dark room.
  • Dictations can run up to 30 minutes, up from 5.
  • The dictation pill opens and closes more smoothly, and it shows the moment you press fn instead of waiting for the microphone.
  • Auto send now also works in apps that take a moment to answer.
  • The meeting panel at the notch no longer shows square corners under its round ones.
  • Fixed a crash that could happen when your AirPods reconnected during a meeting.
  • Updates download in the background and install the next time Ocelli quits. You can turn this off in Settings, under About.

In 2.3.3

  • Dictation is free. No trial, no countdown, in every app.
  • Ocelli for teams: share meeting notes with your team, end-to-end encrypted. Everyone on the team gets Ocelli Pro and Ocelli AI. Start under Settings › Team › Create a team.
  • Teams cost $5 per person a month billed yearly, or $6 month to month, plus tax, for 3 people or more. Add people any time; you pay in your browser through Stripe.

In 2.3.2

  • Smaller updates. Coming from a recent version, Ocelli now downloads only what changed, usually a few MB instead of the whole app.
  • While Ocelli turns your dictation into text, a soft pulse of light runs through the pill.
  • The tick after a dictation has its whole outline again.

In 2.3.1

  • Custom endpoint shows whether your server works, right under its fields, and says so when macOS keeps Ocelli off your local network.
  • Send feedback reaches us again.

New in 2.3

  • The meeting panel can float. Choose Notch or Floating in Settings. On a Mac without a notch it floats on its own.
  • A new voice picture. A dotted weave follows your voice in the dictation pill and while a meeting records.
  • The reminder before a call has one button, Join & Record, that opens the call and starts recording.
  • Discard a meeting with Undo. Nothing is deleted for six seconds.
  • Copy AI answers, in the library and in the meeting chat.
  • With the lid closed, dictation and meetings record from an attached microphone, such as your display's, and tell you so.
  • A note without a title from the AI is named after its summary.
  • Long dictations stay safe while they wait for transcription, and a waiting take is recovered after a restart.
  • Agents can read a meeting while its note is still being written, and can ask for part of a transcript again.
  • Safer: nothing said in a meeting can plant a fake transcript in the note, and an ocelli:// link asks before it replaces your licence.
