Ocelli 2.2.1

Bring in recordings you made elsewhere, and let dictation send for you.

  • Import recordings. Drop an audio or video file on the library window, or choose one in Settings. It becomes a transcript and a note, just like a recorded meeting.
  • Auto Send for dictation. Ocelli can press Return for you after pasting, per app.
  • Dictation starts faster and no longer misroutes takes that contain pauses.
  • Solo recordings are no longer split into two speakers.
  • Summaries retry automatically when the AI service has a hiccup.
  • Cleaner status rows in the sidebar.
