Aside 2.0.2

fixes for notes that saved without a summary, and for dictations that came back half written.

notes

  • a meeting whose note came out blank now contains the summary aside wrote for it.
  • a short meeting gets the same sections as a long one, so a quick call no longer comes back as loose text.
  • a note no longer takes a whole paragraph as its title and its filename.

dictation

  • a long dictation keeps every word: a stretch that came back empty is now recognised again instead of quietly dropped.
  • a loud knock no longer turns the rest of a take quiet, so the speech after it is still heard.

window

  • the reading card's corner now follows the window's own curve, so the gap around it stays even.
