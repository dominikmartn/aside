Aside 1.8.0

rebuilt the settings, added a little license card and fixed a bunch of small things in the library and the notes.

settings

  • settings are flat pages now instead of panels inside panels, and every control sits on the same kind of row.
  • there is a notes tab now, with your name and the vault folder in one place.
  • your license shows up as a card in the license tab.

library and notes

  • deleting from the bottom bar asks the same way the row menu does, and the trash lid closes once you confirm.
  • the note editor no longer draws its text cursor over the library's action bar.
  • lists get one hover that slides along instead of lighting up every row, and round icon buttons always show the arrow pointer.
  • if a summary gets retried, the title gets redone too.
  • the detail page fades in and out, and the transcript player floats above the note now.
  • a few visual fixes in the library: inset rows have their own floor, the banner floor is a material, the editor's edge fade is a proper mask.

transcript and chat

  • the transcript keeps following while it grows, and the live caption stops changing under you while you read.
  • your chat bubble hugs the text instead of going full width, and images in live notes fit the picture, not the row.

under the hood

  • speaker recognition builds on intel macs again.
  • every provider gets the same chat system prompt, so wording can't drift between them.
