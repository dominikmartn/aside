Aside 1.7.9

Meeting notes are a full protocol now, speakers carry their real names, and the transcript starts where your call starts.

Notes

  • A protocol instead of a telegram. Every note now has topics, a chronological details section with transcript timestamps, decisions, and action items with an owner and one line of context.
  • Your name instead of "You". Aside takes it from your calendar or your Mac account; set it yourself under Settings › Notes if you prefer.
  • Rename speakers with one click. Click a speaker label in the transcript to give it a name; the name survives a regenerate.
  • Ask about this meeting answers from the note you have open, not from the last transcript the panel still held.

Recording

  • No more silent first five minutes. When the pre-roll buffer only heard your own microphone, it is dropped instead of being stitched in as silence.
  • Better speaker separation on calls with several remote voices.

Team sync

  • Conflicts keep both versions. When two people edit the same shared note, nothing is overwritten; the conflicting copy waits for you to merge it.
