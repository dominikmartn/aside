Aside 1.7.7

A more resilient microphone path, and for beta testers a dictation that keeps your first words.

Fixed

  • The 5-minute pre-roll no longer gives up. When the microphone was briefly busy while a call started, the pre-roll buffer silently stayed empty. It now retries until it gets the mic, so starting a recording late still brings the minutes before with it.
  • Recording problems tell us why. When the microphone fails mid-recording, Aside now records the exact reason and the state of the audio device — the groundwork for fixing a rare mic dropout some of you have hit.

Dictation (beta builds)

  • Your first words are no longer lost. With Bluetooth headphones, dictation used to miss the opening words while the headset switched profiles — and your music turned muffled. Dictation now listens through the Mac's built-in microphone: no wait, no muffled music.
  • Nothing focused? Nothing lost. Dictating without a text field focused used to vanish the text. It now stays on your clipboard, and the pill says so: press Cmd-V wherever you want it.
  • Calmer, livelier speech bars that follow your voice instead of pumping on their own, and a pill that keeps its shape when you release the key.
  • Dictation gets its own onboarding step. New installs try dictation right in the welcome flow — cursor in a field, hold the key, speak.
